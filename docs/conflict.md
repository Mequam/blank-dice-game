# Conflict and Combat

Conflict occurs when there are extended scenes with apposing forces where time tracking becomes more important. Think combat in traditional TTRPGs.

## Overview

The goal of conflict is to gain three advantages.

Advantages are gained by making a skill check that must minimally beat a set scene difficulty. That skill check is then opposed by whichever forces (typically the defense) are competing with the players in that scene. If the defense fails to beat the attack, that counts as one advantage for the attackers group. Attack wins in a tie.

This continues until the "attacker" fails to beat the scene difficulty, at which point the turn ends and the next character in the defense group becomes the attacker. In any single turn, both the attacker and defender can only use the same skill once.

All skill checks need to make narrative sense, if a player cannot think of a reasonable way to solve a problem on attack or defense that immediately counts as a failed roll for that player.

> TIP:
>
> While conflict often takes the form of a fight between two groups,
> it doesn't have to. Successes could be generated by hacking a computer, giving medical attention, presenting a good argument, driving a car, or any other action that makes sense to give the current side any advantage in this scene.


## Turn Order

The first attacking group is determined by coin flip or by what makes sense narratively (game masters discretion).

The attacker and defender groups then rotate back and forth (switching turns when an attack fails to beat the scene difficulty). Moving clockwise within each group to determine character turn.

In situations with more than two competing groups, groups can be rotated in a circular fashion.

### Stealth and Surprises

One side can sneak up on the other by beating it in an apposed dex v.s. wis roll (any applicable skills added). If the side trying to sneak wins, they get to go first, if they fail standard turn order rules are followed. Sneak rolls can only be made when the players involved have sufficient opportunity to do so, it is up to the game master to determine this.

### Legendary Villains

Certain highly competent enemies might be incredibly hard to get the jump on, these villains go first EVERY time if not caught off guard.

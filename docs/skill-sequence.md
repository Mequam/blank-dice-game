# Skill Sequences

Skill sequences represent action sequences, they are best used when you have a narrative conflict where the apposing
side does NOT have sentience. Think escaping a storm or collapsing building.

## Game Play

At the start of the skill sequence your game master will tell you a starting difficulty and a number of required successes
and potential failures. The goal of the skill sequence is to succeed at the specified number of skill checks before
the failure amount is hit. The game master sets the initial difficulty of the skill sequence, and each additional skill 
check made increases the difficulty by one.

In between every skill check the dungeon master will describe a new problem to deal with that results from the initial problem
prompting the skill sequence (for a skill sequence where you are escaping a burning building, the door being blocked 
by falling wood could be one such one problem). Then any player may attempt to solve that problem, as always
only skills applicable to the problem may be used.

During the skill sequence, each player may only use any given skill once.

### Failure

Failing a skill sequence could mean any number of things depending on the game masters specifications, up to and including
character death. Your game master should tell you AHEAD of time what the consequences of a failed skill sequence will be.

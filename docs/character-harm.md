# Character Harm and Death

Characters in KISS TTRPG can have physical harm inflicted upon them. What follows are the rules for dealing with that harm and potentially killing that character.

> TIP:
>
> These rules are intended for physical harm, but could easily be
> tweaked slightly to simulate other systems (emotional balance for example)
> talk to your game master and as always do what you feel is the most fun!

## Harm and Adrenaline Dice

Characters can have harm inflicted upon them.

When a character is harmed they take an adrenaline dice with faces equal to 3 - the current amount of harm you have.
This dice represents physical harm that your character has undergone.

### Spending Adrenaline Dice

At any time you may use any adrenaline dice you have in any skill check you are making, its faces automatically count towards that skill check. Once used the adrenaline dice is spent, it stays on your character as a marker but may not be used again.

**If you ever spend 3 adrenaline dice, your character dies.**

### Becoming Unconscious

If you are ever harmed 3 times (received 3 adrenaline dice), your character immediately falls unconscious. On your turn you must roll ALL adrenaline dice that are not already spent. Dice that land on their adrenaline face become spent.

Your character can be stabilized by attempting a vitality role of 2 + the number of spent adrenaline dice you currently have, or by an external player attempting an
appropriate skill check of equal difficulty. Adrenaline dice may be used to assist this role, but if in doing so 3 are spent the character immediately dies.
If this check is successful one of the characters unspent adrenaline dice is removed. For the duration of the current scene all actions they make are considered hard.

## Healing and Recovery

Characters may have unspent adrenaline dice removed by resting for a small amount of time in a safe space (~20min, up the game master) or by being healed from any player with an appropriate skill check (game master choice). Only one dice may be removed at a time this way.

Spent dice may only be removed by resting for long periods of time (~24-72 hours, game master choice) or intense surgery with difficulty of at least 3+the number of spent dice. Failing such a surgical check will result in the given player taking an additional harm, many heros have died in such dire straits.

# Vehicle Encounters

Many genres feature shared vehicles that often can be involved in
conflict. These situations while sparse present a unique enough
experience to merit their own suite of rules.

## Vehicle Dice

Vehicles are represented by one to three dice (depending on the vehicle) with faces matching the vehicle attributes. Unlike with player dice, vehicle dice do not receive skills, each attribute fully covers that dice face.

Dice can be assigned faces in any combination, so long as at least
one of each attribute type exists between the two faces.

### Vehicle Attributes

These are the basic vehicle attributes we recommend using,
as always your game master could add or remove to meet the needs
of their game.

- Defense
- Speed
- Power

## Org Dice

Organization dice represent the amount of order among the crew
piloting the vessel and are required to make any action
using the ship.

see [Generate Ship Organization](#generate-ship-organization) for
how org dice are obtained in conflict.

### Layout

Org dice come in two types

#### Basic

These dice have 3 success faces on them.

#### Component Dice

These dice represent extra functionality that your ship might have
installed. In addition to the standard 3 success faces, they can
have up to 3 additional skill faces that represent a components
unique ability it can contribute to conflicts.

When rolled these component faces contribute a success
only in rolls that they can be justified to apply on.

If a component dice has 3 extra faces, one or more of them must
be marked as a cool down face. Once a component dice
hits any cool down face, it may not be used again this encounter.

> GM Tip:
>
> You absolutely can and should have component dice faces with unique
> abilities! Be creative with the dice faces you give your players.

## Vehicle Conflicts

Ship conflict follows the same rules as [standard conflict](./conflict.md),
but with two additions.

### Vehicle Actions

You can use the vehicle to act on the outside world, but only
if there is sufficient organization to do so.

All actions using the vehicle require that an organization dice
be spent to perform those actions. Any number of organization
dice may be selected for this purpose. Each selected dice is
then rolled alongside the player dice and the vehicle dice. All applicable faces are then added to the result.

### Generate Ship Organization

On your turn you can choose to perform your duty on the vessel.

You select how many organization points you want to generate and
describe to the GM what you want to do.

For each organization dice you
want to generate, you make a skill check against the scenes organization difficulty (specified by GM, or defaulted to the scene difficulty) increasing by one after each consecutive check. In between each check the game master will describe some additional problem for you to
solve over the course of generating that order on the ship, ideally related to what you originally intending to do.

If you pass every skill check, each organization dice you want
to generate is generated. If you fail no dice, even ones that would be generated from prior successes, are created.

If component dice would be generated they need to make sense
for the actions that you are taking. You cannot use a cannons
special ability by installing sails.

Note that creating ship organization does NOT generate
an advantage in the conflict.

### Game Master Difficulty Recommendations

Since ship actions will consistently have additional dice added
to them, they have a difficulty scale that is shifted from
standard play. 

If you are going to set a difficulty for generating
org dice, it should typically be lower than the scene difficulty.
For similar reasons, often in vehicle conflict the scene 
difficulty can be higher than you usually set
during standard conflict.

If you are unsure of what to set, set a normal scene difficulty then:

- 2 plus your original number can be the new scene difficulty
- The original number minus 1 can be the org generation difficulty

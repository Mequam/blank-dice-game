# Infiltration

There are often scenarios where time is important, but not quite to the level
of a conflict. Stealth and infiltration are the primary examples of this. These
are basic rules for handling these situations at your table.

## Time Dice and Problems

Time dice represent the movement of the world outside of the players action, they are a reminder
to both the game master and the players that the world around them needs to act.

Time dice are six sided dice that can have between one and five faces on them.

Every time any roll is made by a player during infiltration, time dice are included in that roll.
If the time dice face is up, THE WORLD ACTS, and the game master can present a new 
problem to the players that must be solved (typically with skill checks).
The time dice initiating the world moving is set in the center of the table,
it is not rolled again until a problem generated by time dice is solved
and it leaves table center and re-enters circulation.

> GM Tip:
>
> You absolutely can and should have special time dice with faces on them that have
> unique abilities for areas your players are infiltrating or enemies in a given location.
> When these faces are rolled, special problems or abilities can be triggered. Be creative!


## Infiltration Game Play

Start by having each player describe their approach to the initial infiltration and make a skill
check with that approach.

If at any point the players fail to solve a problem or any check, the game master can choose to 
add any amount or type of time dice into the dice pool, indicating that security has gone up!

This continues until it makes narrative sense that the players would have completed their goal, 
or until there are five or more time dice in play. When that happens, we leave infiltration and
enter a conflict or skill sequence (GM choice).

There is no turn order in infiltration.
Game masters should try and balance each player at the table, but flow freely
between players for what makes sense in the narrative.

Game masters determine the number and type of time dice at the start of infiltration based
on difficulty and location. Standard difficulty is two time dice with 2 faces each.
